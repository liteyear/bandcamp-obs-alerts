01 / CONNECT ONCE

Listen for new purchases

Connect the Gmail account receiving your Bandcamp artist sale receipts. Use a Google app password, rather than your regular password.

Enable 2-Step Verification, then create an app password named “Bandcamp OBS Alerts” at Google App Passwords. If Google doesn’t offer app passwords for this account, stop here; this connection method needs a different account or a different integration.

The credential is saved in this Mac’s Keychain. It grants mail access to that Gmail account; the helper only requests new Bandcamp messages and does not mark them read. Your regular Google password is never needed. Existing mail is skipped each time the helper starts.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Alert preferences

Buyer email addresses, real names from payment records, shipping information, and PayPal fees are never added to the overlay. A public username may itself be a real name.

Ready when you are.

Start “Start Bandcamp Alerts.command” before streaming. Once connected, you can close this browser window and Terminal; the helper keeps running. Purchases are detected when their emails arrive, so email delays can delay alerts.
